## AI Policy mockOnly Reconciliation

The `mockOnly` field appears in two different schemas with two different meanings:

| Location | Schema | Value | Meaning |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|
| Policy `safetyFlags.mockOnly` | `AiPolicy` (tenant-policy.ts:53) | `true` | Admin cannot disable mock-as-default-safety-net. The admin PUT endpoint permanently rejects `mockOnly: false`. |
| Response `safety.mockOnly` | `AiSafetyMetadata` (ai/index.ts:62) | `false` | This specific response was NOT generated by the deterministic mock provider. A real local AI (Ollama) was called. |

**Why Ollama works despite policy mockOnly=true:** The `checkAiPolicy()` method (`support-sessions.service.ts:514`) checks `allowedProviders`, which includes `ollama`. The `safetyFlags.mockOnly=true` policy guard only means "admin can't remove mock-as-safety-net" — it does NOT mean "only mock provider allowed." Local providers (Ollama, LM Studio) are explicitly permitted. Cloud AI is blocked via `cloudCallsAllowed: false` and `localProvidersOnly: true`.

**This is correct, intentional behavior, not a bug.** The two fields coincidentally share the name `mockOnly` but live in different schemas with different semantics. Both are correct in their contexts.
